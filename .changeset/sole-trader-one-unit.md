A sole trader added from a register is kept as one person with their company row: when they cannot be kept, the company row added for them is removed again (whoever asked), and so are its details in the audit entry of its making (as are a half-kept contact's), and every erasure of them (the Object link, the deadline to tell them, the keeping time, a privacy erasure) empties that company row too. Their download carries it, and the mail that tells them says a member of staff added their e-mail address. While people are off, filling in a sole trader's details takes only their number and activity, and a rule's step never takes more.
