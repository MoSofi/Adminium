A company's own page can now show an add-on's action beside Edit. On a table an app declares as companies, an add-on that fills in companies' details (and whose code Adminium loaded) adds its button, in its own words, to the record page; it opens a side sheet with the add-on's screen for that company, full width on a phone. Only someone who may change companies sees the button. Each field a source filled now says where it came from under its value ("From Companies House · 28 Jul"), for anyone who can read the record, until somebody changes that field: then its note goes and the others stay. When a fill is refused because somebody changed a field after the details were looked up, the refusal now names who changed it.
