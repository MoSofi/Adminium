Generated boards, shift rotas, calendars, Gantt charts and roadmaps now look like their designs. A board card shows its link, owner, amount, date and priority, with summary figures above the columns and swimlanes when a table has a category or a team link. A rota shows each person's name, role and end time with an add-shift form. A calendar shows a colour legend and reads only the dates on screen. A Gantt chart has week and month views, grouped rows, progress bars and a today line. A roadmap groups its cards by quarter, with a year switcher. Columns named `start_on`, `due_on`, `starts_at` or `ends_at` now count as a start or end date.
