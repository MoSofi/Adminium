While someone rehearses, a mail about a real row is an ordinary mail again. Before, a row a payments manager made while rehearsing diverted the mail it caused to them, marked `[TEST]`, even when the mail was about a real order (a note or a proof made on it), and a message they queued by hand about a real order went to them too: the real customer never got it. Now a mail goes to the person rehearsing only when the row it is about was made in their rehearsal and is still a sample row; every other mail goes to its real recipient, unmarked, with the payments gate read as payments really are, and is logged like any other.
