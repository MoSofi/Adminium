Card readers at the counter. A new contract, `card-terminal@1` (`createCardTerminal`: credentials, locations, readers, two-step pairing, sending an amount to a reader, its status, cancelling, refunds and their status, events by polling), with its answer schemas, and an in-process demo reader for tests and demos. Studio → Payments → Card readers pairs a reader (the code it shows typed in, or a code shown here typed on it), names and unpairs it. A till puts a record's amount on a reader with `POST /api/v1/payments/terminal/charge` (reading the record and adding a payment to its payments table is enough: a cashier's app role), follows it (`GET …/terminal/charges/:id`), cancels it, and lists the readers (`GET …/terminal/readers`). The engine reads what is due again (a part payment may ask less, never more), holds the reader for one charge at a time, asks the reader every second in short jobs, never gives a charge up blind (a lost send is sent again with the same key; a cancel is followed by a status read, so a card tapped first is a payment; a reader off the network for two minutes is asked to take the amount off), and records the payment like any other: what was asked into the amount, the tip into the payable's `tip` column, with the approval code and the last four. A reader payment's refund goes back through its card reader service, at most what was asked; an Interac payment is refused online (refund it in cash or store credit). A card reader service with no pay page (an add-on providing `card-terminal@1` alone, such as Card readers for Square) is connected in Studio → Payments beside the payment services: its token is saved per mode and checked by the service itself, it has no webhook, no test payment and no live switch (each reader is paired in test or live mode), and it may be connected on the desktop app, since readers are asked outbound. Refunds and disputes made in such a service's own dashboard are read from its events by the payments sweep, hourly while it holds money taken in the last 120 days, and recorded against the reader payment. Studio names each reader service by its add-on's name when pairing, and asks for the location the reader is at from the service's own list (`GET /api/v1/payments/readers/locations`); Stripe needs one.
