A column rule `band` fills a link with the band a number of the row falls in: of the band table's rows within `scope` whose `min` is at most `value`, the one with the greatest `min` (a line's quantity among its product's price breaks; an order's weight among the delivery bands). No row leaves the link empty; past the picked row's `max` the write is refused (`out-of-range` on the number's column, `PUBLIC_WRITE_REFUSED` with that reason through the public API) or, with `over: "empty"`, the link is left empty. A number the write sets is placed before the copies that read through the link; a formula of the row after the formulas; a total of child rows in the parent's settle, so the delivery price follows the order's weight on a create with lines, a line added or removed, and a quote. Only Adminium writes the link.
