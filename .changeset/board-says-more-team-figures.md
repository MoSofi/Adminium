A board page says more. Its page's `config.board` may give each heading a badge read from the column's own row (`columnsFrom.badge`: a stage's odds), a card the name of a linked row, a person by name, an amount, the days since a date as the server counts them and a day (`card`), and the board one sentence above its columns (`note`). A heading with two totals shows both in full. A chart grouped by a staff column, and a list that asks (`people`), call a person by name and never by id, by the people list's rule; somebody it does not name is "Unnamed person". A dashboard card may be a whole team's figures (`"kind": "team-figures"`): counts and sums a person, a target and its cover, answered only to somebody no row limit holds on the tables it reads; anybody else is told the figure is the whole team's and is shown none.
