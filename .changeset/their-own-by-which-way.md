"Their own rows" now says by which way. The columns that decide whose a row is are changed only by somebody the stored row names in one of them, or who leads that person: a helper listed on a row, a cover, or the owner of its parent works on the row and can no longer take it or give it away (403, `not-theirs-to-give`), the hand-over button included. A role's row reach gains `by`: of the rows the person reaches, only the ones a staff column of the table names them in, so an app can let everybody on a deal read its notes and each change only their own. The Rows dialog shows the narrowing and keeps it.
