A value a rule writes from the settings row can name who to write when the setting names nobody: `"else": { "staff": "<column>" }` beside `setting`, a staff column of the rule's record or one link further. An aftercare call is then given to the rep who sold the job when the business has named nobody for aftercare, instead of being left with no owner.
