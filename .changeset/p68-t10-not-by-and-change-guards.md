A role's update reach may say `notBy` a staff column of the row (or of a linked row), so a sales manager changes her team's targets but never her own, on every door, and a record page hides those buttons from her. A column may carry `changes` guards: it changes to some values only from others, only while another column holds a value, and only by (or never by) the person a staff column of the row or of a linked row names, judged on every write but history and refused by name. A rule whose step or e-mail names a code staff never see (`rules.code.hiddenFromStaff`) is refused when it is saved and by the validator, with the column named.
