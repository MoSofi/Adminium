A rule's notice to a role, or to people listed by hand, now goes only to those of them who can open the record it is about: a sales manager whose reach is her own team is no longer told of another team's quote (its number, its values in the title). Nobody left is a line in the run's log, not a failure. A notice about a removed row goes only to people whose read of its table is not limited to some rows.
