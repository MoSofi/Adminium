The add-ons that come with Adminium are now 1.0.26. In the Customiser, a shopper can order a ready-made design again (1.0.24 and 1.0.25 refused every one in the shop), the order-line panel stops reading the shop's set-up again after each save on the dashboard, a words layer kept in a font the shop switched off is no longer refused by the shop's editor, a blank design is named by its kind ("The blank page"), and the Shop settings dialog says its settings cover the shop apps that use this install of the Customiser.
