An add-on that finds companies can now show the mail a found person is sent, as it would go: `GET /api/v1/leads/:key/tell-preview?locale=` draws it for a made-up person at a made-up company, with your business name, postal address, privacy contact, why you keep people and for how long. It sends nothing, and its Object button opens nothing. Anyone who may open the add-on's pages or keep its settings may see it.
