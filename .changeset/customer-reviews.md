Customers can now review what they bought. An app puts a `review` word on the table of the rows reviewed (an order line once its order is shipped, a visit once it is done): the add-on that keeps them, what the row is about and its name, when it may be reviewed, the person the row's claim reaches, how their name is shown, and the column Adminium stamps. A customer who reads the row through their own sign-in or the row's own link asks `GET /public/records/:ref/:id/review` whether it may be reviewed and sends `POST` with whole stars and plain words; Adminium fills everything else, refuses another customer's row, one not yet ready and a second review, and starts the review waiting. An add-on keeps the reviews and their totals (`addOn.reviews`, two tables held to the columns Adminium writes). Staff, the Super Admin included, only move a review's state, give one of four reasons to hide it, and reply, one at a time; nobody creates or deletes one. Adminium keeps the totals of the published reviews in the same transaction as every change, and works them out again when the add-on is installed or updated. A table an add-on shares among its apps may hold an app's role to its own app's rows (`appGrants.appColumn`). A forgotten person's reviews keep their stars and words and lose their name; a person's data lists their reviews, "Download everything" writes them, and the erasure's preview says how many keep their words without the name. Needs 0.3.68.
