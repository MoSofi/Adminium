Someone who manages payments can now rehearse an app's checkout before going live. `PUT /api/v1/payments/rehearsal` switches it on for themselves for four hours (`GET` reads it). While it is on and they are signed in on the app's customer side opened on the staff address, the app's `payments/available` answers `{ online: true, test: true }` for them alone (never kept by a cache, and `test` is on the public client's answer too), and the customer side's config hands them, and only them, the `rehearsal` CSRF token their writes carry; the rows they make there, and on the app's staff screens, are sample rows from the start, entered before the app's mail and rules hear of them (the app's sample list is made when there is none), and a payment started for one is a test link on the address they are on; an app still switched off for real money takes its test payments all the way through the pay page, the checkout and the record. A rehearsal order that could not be entered as a sample row gets no pay link. A test payment on a sample row is now written into the app's own tables like a real one (its payment row joins the sample list, its record is moved to paid and re-recorded), so "Remove sample data" takes it all; a test payment never reaches a row that is not a sample row, and one whose sample row was removed meanwhile ends quietly.
