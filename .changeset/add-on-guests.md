What an app needs to let its shoppers use an add-on's screens. An add-on's public entry may say `appColumn`: through each attached app's key it reads only that app's rows, a create stores that app's key whatever the browser sends, and a change never moves it; a key no app made serves nothing of it. A person's copy may sit on a table of an add-on the app requires (`people[].copies[]` with `addOn`, reached through the app's own link as `"table.column"`), and is found, exported and blanked with them. An add-on's table may `expire` its rows some days after a moment of theirs unless a column holds a value; the server lets them go daily, their files to the bin, keeping any row an app's link still names. The content policy lets a page fetch its own `blob:` URLs, as a 3D viewer on an app's customer side does.
