The add-ons that ship with Adminium are now 1.0.12. Offers & gift cards gives Automations a step, "Issue a voucher": a rule can issue a voucher to the customer of the record it is about, and Offers mails it with its code. Offers and Inventory tell the assistant what their tables are, and offer questions on their own pages.
