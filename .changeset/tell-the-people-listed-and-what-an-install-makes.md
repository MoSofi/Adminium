A rule can tell the people a table lists against its record: `"to": { "listed": "deal_members" }` on a notice, `"to": { "kind": "listed", "table": "deal_members" }` on an e-mail (one each), from an app's manifest or the rule editor. Only those listed who can sign in and open the record when the step runs are told; a suspended or sample person, somebody whose role does not reach the row, and a cover outside its dates are passed over. The install check's Parts card now draws a part that waits for an add-on under that add-on, names the roles the ticks leave with nothing to open, and says what the install will make (tables, roles, rules) worked out from the ticks; the app page's Parts card draws its parts under their add-ons and names the roles left with nothing.
