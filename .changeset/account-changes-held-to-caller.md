Account changes are held to what the person making them holds, and team and row changes to the rows they already reach. An app update keeps the row limits an owner set on its roles, through every version and while an add-on is away. The workspace always keeps a Super Admin who can act. Giving an invited person another role ends their current invitation, so send them a new link afterwards. The Roles page says plainly that "Install and manage apps and add-ons" equals full access, because apps and add-ons run code on the server.
