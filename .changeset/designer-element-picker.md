The Designer's preview has a "Pick an element" button on an app's staff and customer sides. Click it, then click any part of the page: the element shows as a chip above the message box, and the message you send is about that element. The Designer is told its tag and classes, the words it shows, what it sits in and how large it is drawn, and looks for the code that draws it. Esc stops picking.
