The Payment panel now follows what a person may read of a record. Someone whose role reads a record but not its total, or not what is due (a packer who sees an order's items and address and nothing of what it cost), gets no Payment panel on that record and no paid or unpaid badge beside it in an app's list, and may not record a payment by hand on it, since that answer says what is still due. Before, the panel told them the total, what was paid and what was due, whatever their role left out.
