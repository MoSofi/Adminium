The assistant can read its replies to you, with your browser's own voice: nothing is sent anywhere for it. Each reply has a speaker at its end. The panel's header has your own choices (read replies aloud as they arrive, the speed, and a voice where the browser has several for your language), kept with your account.

It reads what the assistant said and one short line for a draft or a change, never the draft or the change itself. It stops on stop, Escape, a new message, closing the panel and the microphone opening, and never starts by itself on a page load. Where the browser has no voice for your language there is no speaker. A workspace can switch it off in Settings → AI → Voice.
