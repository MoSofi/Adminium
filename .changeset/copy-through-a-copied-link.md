A copy through a link that another copy fills is now made when the row is created. In Event Ticketing a ticket copies its show from its ticket type, and its doors time and reminder address through that show; on a real server those came out empty unless the writer sent the show itself, so the release of returned places at the doors and the evening-before reminder had nothing to read. The copies now run in the order their links need, and a copy reads a link filled earlier in the same write as if it had been sent.
