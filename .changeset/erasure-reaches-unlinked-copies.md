An erasure now reaches every copy of the person, not only the declared columns that link to them or hold their address. A copy row with no link back is found by their phone number too (a booking taken on the phone, with no address; a number written with a leading 0 or a country code is the same number), and on a row that is theirs every copy of theirs goes (the name and the phone on a booking found by its address, an unlinked enquiry's name) along with every column marked personal when nobody else is on the row (a stay's note, an enquiry's message). Where a copy links to another person, only what is theirs by a link, the address or the number goes. Tables kept by law stay whole and "What stays" counts their rows there; "What goes" and the person's search and download count and show the rows found this way.
