The add-ons that come with Adminium are now 1.0.27. In the Customiser, a shop's editor takes a ready-made design, and a words layer in the shop's own uploaded font, whatever columns the shop's public pages were opened with: a shop set up on an earlier Customiser and updated could refuse every ready-made design. The order is still checked in full when it is placed.
