The project UI kit (`@adminiumjs/adminium/ui`) gains four parts: `TextArea`, and three that are loaded when first drawn — `Board` (cards in columns, moved by drag or by the keyboard, put back when the save fails), `ImportWizard` (the import wizard for one table) and `MapView` (pins with a list beside them).
