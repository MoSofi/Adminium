With "Link answers to names" off, a survey's invitations hold no time that matches an answer, and the daily forms sweep keeps them that way. Suspending or deleting someone closes their open live connections at once, and a connection that was missed gets no further updates or subscriptions. Suspending or deleting someone also revokes every API key they made, and reactivating them does not bring the keys back, so a shared integration running on one of those keys needs a new key. **This upgrade also revokes, once, every API key made by someone who is suspended now.** A shared integration running on such a key stops working when you upgrade. A revoked key cannot be restored, even by reactivating the person, only replaced with a new key. The audit log lists each key revoked and who made it. A key made by another key is traced back to its person through the audit log. A key with no recorded maker and no such trail (its maker was permanently deleted, or the entry has passed audit retention) cannot be traced to anyone and keeps working, so revoke any key you do not recognise on the API keys page. If you run several replicas, finish replacing all of them before you suspend anyone. Changing your own sign-in address needs a link sent to the new address, opened while signed in, and the old address keeps working until then.
