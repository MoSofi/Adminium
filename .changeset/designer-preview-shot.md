The Designer's preview has a "Take a picture of a part of the page" button on an app's staff and customer sides. Click it, then drag over the part you mean: the picture is attached to your next message, so you can say what is wrong in it before you send. The page in the preview draws the picture itself, so nothing is asked of your screen. Esc stops the drag.
