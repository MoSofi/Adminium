Rules can tell the person a record belongs to: a notice or an email to a staff column of the record (or of a row it links to), once for each spell a row meets the conditions, a summary for each person of their own rows, and a row one step makes read by the steps after it. Each person can set quiet hours and when their summaries are sent; an urgent notice is not held.
