Editing a dashboard now says that its cards show sample numbers. The edit canvas has always drawn every card from made-up data so you can arrange it, but nothing said so, and a dashboard that read 78 on the live page read 44,615 the moment you pressed Edit. The note under the title now reads "The cards show sample numbers while you edit. Your data comes back when you finish editing.", in all eight languages, for a personal layout and for the shared one.
