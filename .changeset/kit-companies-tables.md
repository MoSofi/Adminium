An add-on that finds companies is now told, through its data kit, which tables of its database hold companies: each table's label, the columns a company's number, website, phone, address, activity, about and owner go in, and the table's page the reader may open. The owner may point any of those details at another column of the table; a country is taken only on a column exactly two letters wide, and the owner only on a column that holds a staff member.
