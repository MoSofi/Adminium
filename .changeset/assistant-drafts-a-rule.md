The automation rules page has an Ask assistant button. Describe what should happen and when, and the assistant drafts the rule, shows its steps, and saves it switched off for you to review and turn on. It checks the draft the way the page's own save does, and it is told the page's rules only for someone who may manage automations. This is an early version of the assistant on this page.
