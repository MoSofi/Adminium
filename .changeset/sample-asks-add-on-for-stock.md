An app's sample rows for an add-on (`sampleData.addOns`) may bring the add-on's things and ask it for their stock. A row of a table the add-on's ledger also writes is taken when a role of the add-on may create such a row by hand, without any column the ledger keeps. The file's new `moves` are changes of state made as real saves once its rows are in, so the add-on's own ledger writes what follows (a receipt posted: stock in). What the ledger wrote is listed as the app's sample and leaves with it; a move that is refused undoes the whole file and says why.
