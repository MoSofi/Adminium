The mail that tells a found person where their details came from carries an Object button. It opens a page (in eight languages) that shows their address and one button; pressing it puts the address on the do-not-email list and erases them, so they are never found or written to again. Records of yours that named them (a deal, a quote) stay, with their details taken out, and the page says so. Opening the page changes nothing, and a link changed by one character says "This link doesn't work".
