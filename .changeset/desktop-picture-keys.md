In the desktop app, a Pexels or Unsplash key can be pasted into the Designer's Pictures dialog, and that source appears at once, without a restart. The key is kept with the app's other keys, not in the project's `.env`. Before, the desktop app offered only Openverse and had no way to add these keys.
