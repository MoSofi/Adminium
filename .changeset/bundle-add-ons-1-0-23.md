The add-ons that come with Adminium are now 1.0.23. In the Customiser, an order line's "Make the proof" waits until the design has loaded, and a proof that would leave out every word and picture the shopper chose is refused. Each shop app's owner sets up only its own app's bases and fonts, and each app's staff read only their own customers' designs. The shop's one-colour line, bleed and safe line are kept in a new "Shop settings" dialog on the set-up page. Every card on "Choose a design" shows its own design, and a base's rounded corners can be cleared.
