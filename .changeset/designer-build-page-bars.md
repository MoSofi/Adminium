The Designer's build page has two bars instead of three. The preview has an address bar: it shows the page each side is on, lists the pages of that side, and takes a typed path. The preview is kept while another tab shows, and a reload, a build and a change of side come back to the page you were on. As the window narrows the bar folds, the chat gives up its width, and under about 1,100 pixels the chat and the work area become two views.
