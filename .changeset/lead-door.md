Add-ons that find companies now have a door of their own in Adminium. A person who may open such an add-on's pages can search a register for companies by what they do and where, look up one company's details from the registers they choose and from its own website, add found companies to the app's companies table, fill in ticked details on a company they already keep, and, while the add-on keeps people, find the people who work there and keep the ones they tick. Everything is written as that person, with their own rights. A company already in the table is marked as such even when a colleague keeps it, so nobody adds it twice; a business that asked not to be contacted for sales is never added; a sole trader is added only as a person who is told at once; and a field somebody changed after the details were looked up is never written over. A company's details go only into the columns its app names for them (the `people.lead` manifest word now runs).
