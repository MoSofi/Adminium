A found person is part of a person's data: the privacy page finds them by their address, the download says which source found them and when, the pages it saw their address on, why they were kept and when they were told, and an erasure removes it all, keeping only the day they were told. "How long we keep things" shows a row for the people an add-on found (3 years by default, from when they were found or the last time they wrote); the nightly sweep erases them after that, and erases anyone who could not be told within a month. A found person may be e-mailed only once their consent is recorded with how they agreed and a note; ticking "may be e-mailed" on them is refused. The privacy notice settings take an optional data privacy contact, and say what the notice must name while an add-on keeps the people it finds.
