Someone who opens only an app's own screens (a print shop's counter) may now read `GET /api/v1/add-ons`, and is told only of the add-ons connected to their apps, never their settings' values: a counter's dispatch screen now learns DHL is there and offers "Book a collection". Another app's add-ons, and the other apps an add-on is connected to, stay out of their answer.
