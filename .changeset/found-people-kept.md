An add-on that finds companies may now keep the people it finds at them. Each person is checked first (an address on the do-not-email list, one already in your contacts, a shared inbox such as info@, a missing name or company: not kept), then added to your contacts as the staff member who kept them, and sent a short mail at once saying where their details came from, why they are kept, for how long, their rights, and how to object. Until that mail has gone, no other mail reaches them: not an app's mail, a campaign, a rule's e-mail step or a chat reply. People can only be switched on once the purpose is written, the keeping time chosen, e-mail set up, the public address set and the privacy notice set; a save that tries earlier is refused and says what is missing.
