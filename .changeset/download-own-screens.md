An app you made in a project folder has a Download button on Hosted apps and on the Designer's home. It packs the app at a version no lower than its folder's, and shows the file's fingerprint. The file now carries the screens' source, without build settings, scripts or hidden files, and `adminium app pack` does the same. On another project server, Make it editable here on the app's page copies that source into the project so the Designer can change it; from then on the app comes from that folder.
