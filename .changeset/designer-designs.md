Adminium Designer designs. On the staff and customer sides of an app it now lays out the page for the business, writes a stylesheet of its own (`src/design.css`), draws a logo and writes a short brief (`design.md`) that later turns follow. A design starts from a style: ten are built in (Clean service, Warm table, Bold poster, Soft care, Editorial, Craft market, Night, Bright start, Sharp tech, Classic hotel), chosen by the server from your words, the kind of business or a picture you attach, whose colours are read and set before the model writes anything. Pick one in **Style** on the home page, change it under the chat ("Change the style", was "Change the look"), or add your own: a folder with a `SKILL.md` in `design-skills/`, or a `.zip` by **Add your own…**, on the home page and in the list under the chat. Each built-in style shows a small picture of itself. What a design needs from outside your project (Tailwind CSS, an icon set, fonts from Google's catalogue as packages served by your own server, a site to show pictures from) is asked for on ONE card with a checkbox each, "Select all", "Deselect all" and "Send"; the server finds each version, and never guesses one. The screen build runs Tailwind 4 when the project has it. The Designer finds free pictures (Openverse, or Pexels with your key), you tick the ones to use, and they are copied into the app with their credits. With an Unsplash key, a page's pictures come from Unsplash and are shown from its own site, as its rules ask: the card says so, and your tick allows that site. A font of your own can be attached to a message as a `.woff2` file and used for headings or body text; the app serves it itself. A question with no choices no longer has an "Answer in my own words" button: the answer box is simply there. A new app's key is made from the name the Designer gives it, never from the first words of your request. The check now names a class nothing styles, an emoji used as an icon, a logo that is missing, a colour written where the theme's belongs (a Tailwind palette class too), and text set on a background it does not read on. A side's build takes files from its own app folder and installed packages only. An app made before this release keeps its look exactly as it was until you pick a style. The Designer now looks at the page it built: once a turn's checks are clear, the previewed screen measures what is broken on it (controls that overlap, a picture that did not load, a part wider than the window, a list read without end) and, for a model that reads pictures, draws a picture of itself; the model is told and shown, and fixes what it sees. A page that comes up blank after that is said again, with the browser's own error. What the screen reports is a kind and a count, never its own words. The camera button in the preview's bar switches this off. More that a design is now held to: a component a screen draws, or a helper it calls, and never imports (the page would be blank), a picture shown from an address nothing is at, a table customers may read that no screen reads, a public client made at every render (the page would ask for its rows without end), and a public page with no picture at all. `find_pictures` adds a table's missing picture column itself, looks again with fewer words when a phrase finds nothing, and keeps the small copy the card showed when a picture's own file is too large. Fixed: a sample row's picture was never shown on a customer page (its file was not attached to its row); the first visitor to a page of new pictures was refused some of them ("Busy") and saw empty frames, since a browser asks for a picture once: they now wait their turn. The starter's first screen (`.hero`) no longer sets its own width, so it can be a band across the window. Sample rows added before this release show their pictures on a customer page once the sample data is removed and added again. The build page now follows a new app's key the moment the Designer names it, so the preview shows during the first build instead of only once the turn ends. A key sent to a picture source no longer follows a redirect to another host.
