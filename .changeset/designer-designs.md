Adminium Designer designs. On the staff and customer sides of an app it now lays out the page for the business, writes a stylesheet of its own (`src/design.css`), draws a logo and writes a short brief (`design.md`) that later turns follow. A design starts from a style: ten are built in (Clean service, Warm table, Bold poster, Soft care, Editorial, Craft market, Night, Bright start, Sharp tech, Classic hotel), chosen by the server from your words, the kind of business or a picture you attach, whose colours are read and set before the model writes anything. Pick one in **Style** on the home page, change it under the chat ("Change the style", was "Change the look"), or add your own: a folder with a `SKILL.md` in `design-skills/`, or a `.zip` by **Add your own…**. What a design needs from outside your project (Tailwind CSS, an icon set, fonts from Google's catalogue as packages served by your own server, a site to show pictures from) is asked for on ONE card with a checkbox each, "Select all", "Deselect all" and "Send"; the server finds each version, and never guesses one. The screen build runs Tailwind 4 when the project has it. The Designer finds free pictures (Openverse, or Pexels with your key), you tick the ones to use, and they are copied into the app with their credits. A question with no choices no longer has an "Answer in my own words" button: the answer box is simply there. A new app's key is made from the name the Designer gives it, never from the first words of your request. The check now names a class nothing styles, an emoji used as an icon, a logo that is missing and a colour written where the theme's belongs. A side's build takes files from its own app folder and installed packages only. An app made before this release keeps its look exactly as it was until you pick a style.
