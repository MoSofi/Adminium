A role's reach of a table that hangs off a parent may say `via` the link and `by` a staff column of the parent, so a sales manager reaches the visits of the deals her team owns and none on another team's deal her rep helps on, on every door (the list and the record, a change, a new row, a dashboard figure, the live frames and the role editor's count). A hand-over button may say what it `carries`: the old owner's child rows (a deal's booked visits, its open follow-ups) go to the new owner in the same write, each as its own update judged by the listing rule, leaving made visits and colleagues' rows, with no Undo. The listing rule now also judges a person's own change and new row as their rules fill them, so a stamp that puts somebody in a listing's person column is refused when they could not open what it lists them on. A carried row of a table that takes a resource for a time is placed under its day's lock, so the new person's clash refuses the hand-over.
