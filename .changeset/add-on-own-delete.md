A shopper may delete their own saved draft through its own link. An add-on's own link entry (`claim.own: true`) may also hold `DELETE`, held to the same `writableWhen` states as its change, so an ordered design is never removed by its shopper; the removal asks no reason, reaches no row but the link's own, and sends the row's files to the bin as a staff delete does. `DELETE` on any other manifest entry is refused. A public removal through a hand-made key is now held to its endpoint's `writable_when` too, and sends the row's files to the bin.
