# Design checklist

The short form of `SKILL.md`. Hold every screen people see to it.

- **A design the person brought** (an HTML template, a static site): keep it exactly. Only the
  access items of "Every screen" apply to it, and the author's credit line stays.

- **Whose page, what it offers, what to do next**: plain within three seconds. A header with the
  logo and name, three to five links and ONE main button. A first screen that says what the
  business is in a customer's words, with the concrete promise under it. Never "Welcome" alone.
- **Order of a public page**: first screen, what is on offer (with prices), why to trust it
  (the place, hours, a quote), the form, a footer with address, hours and contact.
- **Hierarchy**: three or four sizes, clearly apart. The important thing big, dark and first.
  Text lines of 45 to 75 characters, 16 px or more.
- **Space**: little inside a group, more between groups, most between sections. Cramped looks
  cheap. Align everything to one grid.
- **Colour and type**: only the theme's values. The accent for the main action and a few marks.
  One band in another colour gives a page rhythm. Two fonts: the theme's heading and body.
- **Pictures**: a page about food, rooms, people or things shows them. Each is a spot
  (add_spots) the person fills; their own attached picture goes in with fill_spot; never
  another site's address on your own.
  Every picture a fixed shape, and alt text.
- **Icons**: from the icon set, one size. Never an emoji as an icon.
- **Logo**: every public side has one, `assets/logo.svg`, in the header.
- **Words**: what this business would write. Buttons say what happens ("Book a table"). After
  sending, say what happens next. An empty list says so.
- **Every screen**: works on a phone first; labels stay visible; focus is visible; light and dark
  both right.
- **Not this**: a gradient hero with centred text, three equal cards with an emoji each, the same
  round corner and soft shadow on every box, a page all one size and one grey.
- **Staff screens**: the work first. No hero, no pictures, no bands.
- **Dashboard pages are Adminium's own**: do not restyle them.
