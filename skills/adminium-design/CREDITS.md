# Credits

The guidance in this skill was written for Adminium. It draws on ideas from these works, each
under the MIT licence:

| Work | What was learned from it |
|---|---|
| Owl-Listener, `designer-skills` (`ui-design`, `visual-critique`) | Hierarchy, spacing and how to judge a screen |
| Owl-Listener, `inclusive-design-skills` | The access basics every screen is held to |
| elayadesign, `landing-page-design` | The order of a public page and its first screen |
| MengTo, `Skills` (`no-ai-design-slop`) | What generic machine-made design looks like, to avoid it |

No text of theirs is copied here. The ready-made parts an app can take (`button`, `card`, `dialog`
and the others) are written after the parts of shadcn/ui (MIT).

The fonts the styles name are from Google's catalogue, each under its own open licence, and are
installed as `@fontsource/<family>` packages.
