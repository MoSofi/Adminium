# The built-in styles

| Key | Title | For |
|---|---|---|
| `clean` | Clean service | Cool white, slate and a clear blue. For clinics, law and accounting offices, and anything that should feel orderly. |
| `warm` | Warm table | Cream, terracotta and a serif for headings. For restaurants, cafés, bakeries and anything about food. |
| `bold` | Bold poster | Black on white, one red, heavy type and square corners. For events, gyms, clubs and music. |
| `calm` | Soft care | Green-grey, a soft serif and room to breathe. For wellness, beauty, yoga and therapy. |
| `editorial` | Editorial | Paper white, ink and thin rules, like a magazine. For studios, architects, photographers and writers. |
| `craft-market` | Craft market | Oat, charcoal and clay with a friendly serif. For makers, small shops, florists and potters. |
| `night` | Night | Near-black with amber and tall condensed headings. For bars, cinema, late venues and tattoo studios. |
| `bright-start` | Bright start | Warm white, navy and a sunny yellow with round friendly type. For schools, nurseries, clubs and community groups. |
| `sharp-tech` | Sharp tech | White, graphite and electric violet with a geometric heading font. For software, agencies, repair and IT. |
| `classic-hotel` | Classic hotel | Ivory, navy and brass with an elegant serif. For hotels, venues, weddings and estates. |
