# Night

Near-black with amber and tall condensed headings. For bars, cinema, late venues and tattoo studios.

Headings in Bebas Neue, text in Manrope. The colours, corners, shadow and spacing are in `theme.json`; use
them through the theme's values, never as written colours.

## The first screen

A dark header with the name in tall capitals and an amber button. The hero is a tall condensed heading in capitals over a dark photograph with a black fade, with tonight's hours or the next showing in amber.

## What is on offer

What is on tonight as rows: the time in amber, the title in tall capitals, a line of detail, a button. Drinks or films as dark cards with a picture on top. The magenta second colour marks one thing only: sold out, last seats, tonight.

## Pictures

Low light, warm lamps, glass, neon. Dark enough for white text to sit on with a fade. Wide.

## The logo

The name in the condensed capitals, perhaps inside a thin amber rule box, like a sign.

## Do

- Keep the page dark in both light and dark settings.
- Set headings in capitals with a little letter spacing.
- Use amber for times, prices and the main button.

## Do not

- No white sections.
- No pale grey text on the dark ground smaller than 15 px.
- No more than two glowing things on a screen.
