# Bright start

Warm white, navy and a sunny yellow with round friendly type. For schools, nurseries, clubs and community groups.

Headings in Baloo 2, text in Nunito. The colours, corners, shadow and spacing are in `theme.json`; use
them through the theme's values, never as written colours.

## The first screen

A cheerful header with a round logo, clear links and a yellow "Join" or "Enrol" button with navy text. The hero has a big round heading, one warm sentence, the button, and a photograph in a very round frame with one or two simple shapes (a circle, a wavy line) behind it in sky blue and yellow.

## What is on offer

Classes or groups as large round cards with a coloured top, an icon, the age or the day, and the time. A navy band holds the practical facts parents need: hours, ages, where, how to join. The form is short and friendly.

## Pictures

Children and groups doing things, bright daylight, faces. Round or very rounded frames.

## The logo

A round badge with a simple sun, star or book and the name in the round font.

## Do

- Use large type and large buttons.
- Use the blue and yellow together, with navy for text.
- Say the practical facts early: ages, days, times.

## Do not

- No thin grey text.
- No sharp corners.
- No more than two decorative shapes on a screen.
