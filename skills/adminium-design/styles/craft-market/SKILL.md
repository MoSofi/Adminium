# Craft market

Oat, charcoal and clay with a friendly serif. For makers, small shops, florists and potters.

Headings in DM Serif Display, text in DM Sans. The colours, corners, shadow and spacing are in `theme.json`; use
them through the theme's values, never as written colours.

## The first screen

A header with the logo, the shop's sections and a basket or "Order" button. The hero shows the best product large on the oat background, with a short friendly line about what is made and by whom, and a button to the shop.

## What is on offer

Products in a grid of cards, three or four across: a square picture, the name, one detail (the material, the size), the price, and a small button. A forest-green band tells the maker's story in a few lines with a picture of the hands or the workshop.

## Pictures

The product alone on a plain light ground, square; and the making of it: hands, tools, the bench. Natural light.

## The logo

A small stamp: a round or oval badge with a drawn object of the trade and the name around or beside it, in clay.

## Do

- Show the price and the main detail on every card.
- Use small badges for "new", "last one", "made to order".
- Tell who makes it, with a face or hands.

## Do not

- No glossy gradients.
- No more than four products in a row.
- No picture with a busy background.
