# Warm table

Cream, terracotta and a serif for headings. For restaurants, cafés, bakeries and anything about food.

Headings in Playfair Display, text in Inter. The colours, corners, shadow and spacing are in `theme.json`; use
them through the theme's values, never as written colours.

## The first screen

A header with the logo, links to the menu, the story and contact, and a "Book a table" button. The hero is a large serif line about the food, a short second line with the place and hours, the button, and a wide photograph of the dining room or a signature dish.

## What is on offer

The menu in sections (starters, mains, desserts), each dish a row or card with a square picture, the name in the serif, one line of what is in it, and the price at the end of the row. A dark brown band holds a guest's quote. The booking form sits in a card beside the hours and the address.

## Pictures

Food seen close and warm, from above or at table height; the room with people in it. Dishes square (1:1), the room wide (16:9). Rounded corners as the theme has them.

## The logo

A small drawn mark of the trade (a fork, a leaf, a cup) or the first letter in a circle, in terracotta, beside the name in the serif.

## Do

- Let the serif headings be large and the text small and quiet.
- Put prices in the same place on every row.
- Use the olive second colour for small marks: vegetarian, new, seasonal.

## Do not

- No bright white backgrounds: the cream is the page.
- No more than one dark band.
- No photo without a fixed shape.
