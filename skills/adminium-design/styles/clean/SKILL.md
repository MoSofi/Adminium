# Clean service

Cool white, slate and a clear blue. For clinics, law and accounting offices, and anything that should feel orderly.

Headings in Inter, text in Inter. The colours, corners, shadow and spacing are in `theme.json`; use
them through the theme's values, never as written colours.

## The first screen

A calm header with the name and a phone number or a booking button. The hero is a clear sentence about what the practice does and for whom, with one button, on the plain background or beside one honest photograph of the place or the people.

## What is on offer

Services as a tidy grid of cards, each with a small line icon, a name and one sentence; prices or durations in a quiet row. Opening hours and the address in a plain two-column block.

## Pictures

Real people and rooms in daylight, cut wide (16:9 or 3:2), no heavy filters. No stock handshakes.

## The logo

A letter or a simple geometric mark in a rounded square of the accent blue, beside the name in the heading font, weight 700.

## Do

- Keep lots of white space and straight alignment.
- Use the blue for the one main button and for links only.
- Show trust plainly: qualifications, years, hours, a phone number.

## Do not

- No gradients, no decorative shapes.
- No more than one accent colour on a screen.
- No playful fonts or all-caps headings.
