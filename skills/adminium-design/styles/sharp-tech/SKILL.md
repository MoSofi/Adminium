# Sharp tech

White, graphite and electric violet with a geometric heading font. For software, agencies, repair and IT.

Headings in Space Grotesk, text in Inter. The colours, corners, shadow and spacing are in `theme.json`; use
them through the theme's values, never as written colours.

## The first screen

A compact header with the logo, links and a violet button. The hero is a tight, large heading that states the result for the customer, one sentence under it, two buttons (one violet, one quiet), and a neat drawing of the product or a framed screenshot instead of a photograph.

## What is on offer

What is offered as a grid of small cards with a line icon, a short title and one sentence; then a row of three numbers or facts; then prices or steps as a clean table. A graphite band carries the last call to action.

## Pictures

Screenshots in a thin frame, simple diagrams, or the device being repaired on a plain ground. Few photographs.

## The logo

A small geometric mark (a square cut by a line, two shapes meeting) in violet, beside the name in the heading font.

## Do

- Keep text short and exact; lead with the result.
- Use the mono font for small labels and numbers.
- Use the lime only as a tiny status mark.

## Do not

- No purple-to-blue gradient hero.
- No stock photos of people at laptops.
- No more than six cards in a section.
