# Bold poster

Black on white, one red, heavy type and square corners. For events, gyms, clubs and music.

Headings in Archivo Black, text in Archivo. The colours, corners, shadow and spacing are in `theme.json`; use
them through the theme's values, never as written colours.

## The first screen

A header in black with the name in heavy capitals and one red button. The hero is a very large heading of a few words, set tight, that may run to the edges, over a black band or a strong photograph in black and white, with the date or the offer in the yellow second colour.

## What is on offer

What is on (classes, dates, line-up) as a list of heavy rows with a thick line between them: the day or time large at the start, the name, and a button at the end. Cards have a thick border and no shadow.

## Pictures

People in motion, high contrast, black and white or one colour washed over. Cut wide or very tall. No soft lifestyle pictures.

## The logo

The name itself as a wordmark in the heavy font, all capitals, or its initials in a black square.

## Do

- Make headings much larger than feels safe.
- Use thick lines (2 to 3 px) to divide, not boxes with shadows.
- Keep to black, white, the red and a little yellow.

## Do not

- No rounded cards, no soft shadows.
- No thin or light type.
- No more than a few words in a heading.
