# Classic hotel

Ivory, navy and brass with an elegant serif. For hotels, venues, weddings and estates.

Headings in Cormorant Garamond, text in Lato. The colours, corners, shadow and spacing are in `theme.json`; use
them through the theme's values, never as written colours.

## The first screen

A quiet header: the name in the serif in the middle or at the start, links in small spaced capitals, and a brass-outlined "Check availability" button. The hero is a full-width photograph of the house with a short serif line over a soft navy fade, and a slim bar under it for dates and guests.

## What is on offer

Rooms as large cards with a wide picture, the room's name in the serif, three facts in a row (guests, bed, view), the price a night, and a button. A navy band with ivory text tells the house's story or holds a guest's words. Small labels in spaced capitals with a short brass rule beside them.

## Pictures

The building, the rooms made up, the view, details of linen and breakfast. Wide (3:2 or 16:9), evening or morning light, almost square corners.

## The logo

A monogram of one or two letters in the serif inside a thin brass ring or frame, with the name under or beside it in spaced capitals.

## Do

- Use the brass for thin rules, small labels and the main button only.
- Set headings in the serif at a generous size, weight 500 to 600.
- Leave wide margins; centre short texts.

## Do not

- No bright colours.
- No heavy or condensed type.
- No busy grids: two rooms across at most.
