# Editorial

Paper white, ink and thin rules, like a magazine. For studios, architects, photographers and writers.

Headings in Newsreader, text in Inter. The colours, corners, shadow and spacing are in `theme.json`; use
them through the theme's values, never as written colours.

## The first screen

A thin header: the name as a wordmark at the start, a few small links at the end, a hairline under it. The hero is one very large serif sentence set to the left over two or three lines, with a small label above it and a single large picture below, edge to edge.

## What is on offer

Work as a list of projects with a number, a title in the serif and a year, divided by hairlines, or as a grid of large pictures with a small caption under each. Text in a narrow column, as in a magazine. No cards, no boxes, no shadows.

## Pictures

The work itself, large and uncropped where possible; otherwise 3:2. No rounded corners.

## The logo

A wordmark: the name in the serif, or in small spaced capitals in the sans. No symbol.

## Do

- Use hairlines (1 px) and space to divide.
- Set small labels in spaced capitals above headings.
- Let one picture be very large rather than many small.

## Do not

- No cards, no shadows, no rounded corners.
- No coloured backgrounds except one ink band.
- No icons as decoration.
