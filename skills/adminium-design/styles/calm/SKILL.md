# Soft care

Green-grey, a soft serif and room to breathe. For wellness, beauty, yoga and therapy.

Headings in Fraunces, text in Nunito Sans. The colours, corners, shadow and spacing are in `theme.json`; use
them through the theme's values, never as written colours.

## The first screen

A light header with the name and a "Book" button. The hero is a gentle serif line, one soft sentence and the button, beside a tall photograph with very round corners, on the pale green-grey.

## What is on offer

Treatments as wide soft cards or a simple list: the name, how long it takes, one line of what it does, the price. A pale band holds a short promise or a client's words. The booking form is short and has large fields.

## Pictures

Soft daylight, hands, plants, fabric, calm faces. Tall (3:4 or 4:5) with very round corners. Nothing clinical.

## The logo

A simple leaf, drop or circle drawn with one line, in the sage green, beside the name in the serif at weight 500.

## Do

- Give everything more space than seems needed.
- Keep type light: 400 to 500 for headings.
- Use the blush second colour sparingly, for one small mark.

## Do not

- No hard black, no heavy weights.
- No sharp corners.
- No crowded grids of more than three across.
